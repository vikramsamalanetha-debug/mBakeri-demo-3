The designer

Chris Foster

He calls M Bakeri an intuitive space, where new dishes come to life by trusting instinct.

Chris grew up around his parents' passion for food, design and art, and years of travel shaped his palate. He modeled, then founded a live-entertainment production company known for big shows.

M Bakeri is the show he chose to keep running: a bakery where every cake, tart and quiche is a reflection of the person who made it.

How a piece makes the collection

Like a look before a show, every item passes through Chris before it is sold.
